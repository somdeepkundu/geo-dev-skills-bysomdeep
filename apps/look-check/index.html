Look Check

Is the camera direction and tilt right? Aim at a landmark and see.

not started

This page reads the phone's compass, tilt and GPS with the phone-field-sensors skill, then compares the camera direction with the true bearing to a landmark you pick on the map.

  1. Tap Start sensors and allow motion, location and camera.
  2. Tap a far landmark on the map (a tower, a building corner, a tree line), ideally 100 m or more away.
  3. Point the camera at it, hold still, tap Record aim. Repeat from a few spots.

Nothing leaves the phone. Readings stay in this browser until you export them.
